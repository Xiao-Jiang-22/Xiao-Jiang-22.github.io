I co-founded Holoscale, Inc. in 2026 to develop advanced X-ray imaging software for industrial CT. Our software is designed to improve reconstruction accuracy and support faster, more flexible scanning across conventional and robotic CT systems, helping meet modern manufacturing’s demand for reliable, high-throughput inspection. We are developing our technology with support from the Maryland Innovation Initiative (MII) and the Johns Hopkins President’s Venture Fellowship (PVF).
