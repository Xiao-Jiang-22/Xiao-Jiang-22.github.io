I’m Xiao Jiang (姜潇), an X-ray imaging researcher and engineer. I am a co-founder of **[Holoscale, Inc.](https://holoscale.co/)** and a postdoctoral researcher at **[Johns Hopkins School of Medicine](https://www.hopkinsmedicine.org/som)**, based in Baltimore, Maryland. I earned my Ph.D. in **Biomedical Engineering** at **Johns Hopkins** and studied **Physics** at the **[University of Science and Technology of China](https://en.ustc.edu.cn/)**. More about my background is in my [CV]({{ '/cv/' | relative_url }}).
